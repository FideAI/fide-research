## Join this task

Comment with the part you can help with, your availability, and a proposed first
output. The coordinator will check overlaps and agree the next step before
substantive work. A task does not reserve the whole call.

## Updates and completion

Keep scope decisions and outputs in this issue. Link code or scientific artifacts
from their owning repository. When the contribution ends, state the actual
review status and acknowledge contributors. A pilot, negative result, or paused
contribution does not establish a finding or complete its parent research call.
