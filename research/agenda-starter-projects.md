# Contributor starter projects

Each project below offers one small artifact a collaborator can draft or review
using public or synthetic material. The effort ranges are estimates for the
first artifact. Use the [research-task template](../templates/research-task.md)
when a maintainer confirms scope, support, credit, and a review route.
Preparation tasks use public records or fictional cases. A study with participants
needs an agreed protocol, consent process, and reviewer before recruitment.

Check the **Related tasks** link in the call brief. If a task matches, use that
issue to offer your first output and rough availability. If none matches, use
[Propose a contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml)
with the call ID, the artifact you want to draft or review, and any support
you need. Each brief also links to its GitHub Discussion for questions,
reading notes, and offers to help. Keep public contributions to shareable

[contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md#find-or-scope-concrete-work).

## Sharing a first draft

Choose one row in the table and spend the first 15 minutes on its small first
step below. The time estimate covers a draft of the first artifact. Stop at
your time limit and share what you have, including an exact blocker if needed.
For the eight entries from FID-045 through FID-099 in this guide, a Markdown
note is enough. A second reviewer can be requested when you submit it.

Use the call's Discussion for a question, partial draft, or offer to review.
Check its related tasks before opening a contribution proposal. Include the
call ID, public source links or fictional inputs, what you produced, time spent,
and one specific question for a reviewer. Alex Chao or the call's coordinator
checks overlaps and agrees scope and review support before substantive work.
The completion criteria below describe a reviewable draft. Acceptance and a
finding require their respective review.

GitHub posting requires an account. You can also share the call ID and a short
contribution offer through [Participate](https://fideai.org/participate/).
The [contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md#start-here-join-a-research-call)
explains the short private introduction, completed once across calls. Keep
contact details and confidential constraints in that private route.

## FID-045

[Faith-AI Research Gap Map and Evidence Commons](../ideas/FID-045-faith-ai-research-gap-map.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/171) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-045%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-045&idea=FID-045&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F171)

**First 15 minutes.** Create a two-row table with each paper's prompt context, measured outcome, and reference judgment. Fill one cell with a section citation.

**Start with the two-source review.** Read the methods and limitations of
[Omissive Bias](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24319v1) and
[When AI Takes Sides](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22975v1), using the existing
[omission review](reviews/2605.24319-omissive-bias.md),
[conversion review](reviews/2605.22975-conversion-bias.md), and
[source-note template](../templates/source-note.md).
The first measures religious representation in everyday ethical answers. The
second measures support for hypothetical faith transitions. Check the
[living-review guidance](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-21)
for how to record search dates and reasons to update a conclusion.

**Draft one decision brief.** Ask what evidence would justify adding a religious
perspective to a response rubric given the user's stated context. Record each
study's prompt context, outcome, reference judgment, population, version, and
limitation with section citations. State one conditional rubric decision and one
unresolved comparison. Map the papers to available artifacts, existing review
notes, and a next task with its access or reviewer requirement. Compare the
brief's proposed usefulness with a bibliography containing the same references.

**Done** means a faith-domain reviewer can trace each claim to evidence and
challenge its interpretation. Include the search date, inclusion rule for this
two-study pilot, one finding that would change the recommendation, and a reason
to stop maintaining it if the decision or evidence stream goes quiet. Treat
new evidence as a trigger for manual review. Extend to a reviewer comparison
only after the brief and bibliography have matched source coverage. If the
brief adds no decision value, retain the source notes and retire that format.

## FID-069

[Verifiable Delegation and Revocation in Multi-Agent Networks](../ideas/FID-069-verifiable-delegation-revocation-multi-agent-networks.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/195) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-069%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-069&idea=FID-069&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F195)

**First 15 minutes.** Write one reference rule and one authorized trace before adding forbidden composition and revocation variants.

**Start with specification limits.** Read the verifier and specification sections
of [Scalable Formal Oversight](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/SfhFh9Hfm6JYvzbby/the-scalable-formal-oversight-research-program).
Use a fictional charity and an independent service provider. One agent may read
synthetic record fields and another may send approved summaries. Specify an
end-state rule forbidding a summary that combines two restricted fields.

**Write four traces.** Include an individually permitted sequence that produces
the forbidden combination, a queued send executed after its authority is revoked,
a permitted summary, and a case where the combined-field rule is missing from
the verifier's specification. Record actor, delegated scope, policy version,
queue and execution times, and resulting state. Compare per-tool checks with
an execution-time check of current authority and the combined effect. Keep an
independent reference policy for identifying the omitted invariant.

**Done** means a second reviewer can derive expected allow or block decisions
for all four traces and identify what each checker misses or blocks needlessly.
List trusted components, including identity, policy, clock, and dispatch state.
A text fixture and truth table suffice. A later implementation should handle
retries and concurrent dispatch. If existing per-tool controls already enforce
the full rule at execution, document that result before adding a new verifier.

## FID-074

[Agent Alignment and Runtime Assurance](../ideas/FID-074-agent-alignment-runtime-assurance.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/200) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-074%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-074&idea=FID-074&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F200)

**First 15 minutes.** Copy the fictional policy and trace table below into a note. Expand one row into a structured log and the same facts in a short evidence packet.

**Start with a control protocol.** Read the main-task, side-task, monitor, and
intervention setup in the
[ControlArena introduction](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/introducing-controlarena-a-library-for-running-ai-control-experiments).
Use a fictional access-provisioning workflow with one operator authorized to
pause a queued permission change before it takes effect.

**Draft a matched fixture.** Write a policy deviation, a benign lookalike, and
a correctly authorized change. Freeze the agent actions and monitor scores.
For each trace, prepare a score-only view, a conventional structured log, and
an evidence packet organizing the same log facts into authority, chronology,
and recovery options. Match the latter two views on facts and access. Specify
a fixed review budget, the irreversible step, the justified intervention, and
what a deterministic permission gate would already stop.

**Worked fictional example.** Policy P1 permits the coordinator to grant read
access. A write grant requires a current security-owner approval. The review
operator may pause any queued grant. Larger scores indicate more concern.
These invented scores illustrate matched views and are separate from
measurements of a model.

| Trace | Queued action | Current approval | Monitor score | Reference decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Coordinator grants read access | Coordinator approval A1 | 0.2 | Release |
| R2 | Coordinator grants write access | Coordinator approval A2 only | 0.7 | Pause |
| R3 | Coordinator grants write access | Security-owner approval A3 | 0.7 | Release |

For R2, record the actor, requested write scope, approval issuer, policy version,
queue time, and planned execution time in both evidence views. A current-permission
gate should already block it. Use the equal scores in R2 and R3 to check whether
the authority evidence justifies different decisions. Create separate evaluation
cases before a participant comparison.

**Done** means an independent security or operations reviewer can audit the
expected pause, correct, release, or escalate decision from the fixture. List
missed deviations, needless stops, completed work, and review time as proposed
outcomes. First qualify the rubric and trace completeness. A participant
comparison needs separate review. If the packet adds no value over structured
logs at the same budget, keep the simpler presentation. Record cases where a
deterministic gate makes operator review redundant.

## FID-077

[Independent Agent Incident Investigation and Evidence Sufficiency](../ideas/FID-077-independent-agent-incident-investigation.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/203) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-077%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-077&idea=FID-077&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F203)

**First 15 minutes.** Write a search window and inclusion rule, then inspect one candidate report. Spend at most 30 minutes finding a matching report before recording the search and access blocker.

**Start with one public evidence record.** The
[AI Incident Database snapshot and citation guide](https://incidentdatabase.ai/research/snapshots/)
provides a discovery frame. Choose one report of an agent claiming completion
without corroborating execution. Record the exact incident URL and access date,
or the snapshot filename and hash if using a download. Follow the discovery
report to public traces or a first-party postmortem where available.

**Draft one case card and a replay plan.** Fix the search dates and inclusion
rule, record excluded candidates and duplicate event IDs, and separate the
completion claim, observed execution evidence, inferred cause, and unknown
outcome. Give section or trace references and record corrections. Ask a second
reviewer whether the records support any specific control gap. If they do,
translate it into a fictional trace with independent ground truth and a
benign control. If they do not, record the evidence blocker.

**Done** means another investigator can check the evidence classification and
either reproduce the synthetic failure or explain the missing evidence needed
to construct it. Publish original summaries and references, with a privacy and
disclosure review before releasing a named case. Keep source text within its
reuse terms. Report the bounded search coverage. A broader case series needs
multiple deduplicated events and agreement checks before calling a gap recurring.
Insufficient corroboration is a valid first output.

## FID-078

[When Trustworthiness Evaluations Transfer Across Domains](../ideas/FID-078-cross-domain-evaluation-validity.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/204) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-078%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-078&idea=FID-078&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F204)

**First 15 minutes.** Use the linked FID-074 example to name one permission change and write the correct decision before and after it.

**Start with one decision and independent ground truth.** Read
[METR's elicitation guidance](https://metr.org/blog/2024-03-15-guidelines-for-capability-elicitation/)
for tool and scorer confounds, then use the
[FID-074 worked permission-change example](#fid-074). Specify whether a reviewer should expand, hold, or revalidate that
workflow after one permission rule changes.

**Write a transfer test specification.** Define an original rubric and a frozen
workflow-aware alternative. Make paired development cases and reserve a separate
test set that varies one factor, such as whether an approval survives a role
change. Hold the underlying obligation and task difficulty constant. Set a
decision threshold before opening the test cases. Compare the original score,
the alternative, and a simple current-permission check against action outcomes
adjudicated independently of all three scores. Include authorized and unauthorized
actions and a missing-record case requiring deferral.

**Done** means another evaluator can identify the held-out cases, thresholds,
reference decisions, and proposed missed-failure and false-alarm counts. A
one-page protocol and a few fictional examples suffice for the first review.
Report an uninformative or failed transfer if the independent endpoint cannot
be scored or the simple baseline suffices. Test data and a reviewed analysis
plan are needed before estimating effects. This first fixture concerns one
replay change. Broader cross-domain claims require the parent call's held-out
domain comparison and qualified reviewers.

## FID-099

[How Does Training Shape Behavioral Dispositions?](../ideas/FID-099-training-and-behavioral-dispositions.md)

[Discuss or share a draft](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/discussions/225) · [Related tasks](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+%22FID-099%22+in%3Atitle) · [Propose this contribution](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/new?template=claim-research-idea.yml&title=Contribution%3A+FID-099&idea=FID-099&related-issue=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FFideAI%2Ffide-research%2Fdiscussions%2F225)

**First 15 minutes.** Write a correct commented and uncommented implementation of the same harmless function. Record that both pass the functionality checks before designing the reward comparison.

**Start with the measurement and limitations.** Read Sections 3 and 7 of
[Contrastive Belief Updates](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18966v1).
Its measurement requires checking that the intended expectation changed.
A separate probe about the expected grader preference should test that change,
independently of the task output used to measure behavior. For
one harmless coding task, fix the requested functionality and vary the reported
grader preference for a neutral style feature such as a comment convention.

**Draft a comparison and access check.** Specify opposing grader-expectation
conditions, a matched factual update unrelated to reward, and a direct instruction
condition. State which outputs satisfy the task independently of the style score.
For a checkpoint comparison, name a publicly available checkpoint pair, license,
training difference, compute estimate, and release permissions, or record the
access blocker. Check changes to task, tool-use, and instruction-following
competence. Hold out evaluation wording and specify independent manipulation
checks before interpreting a null result. A prompt-only dry-run can qualify
materials, while a training-effect claim requires the controlled checkpoint study.

**Done** means a training or evaluation reviewer can challenge the comparison,
confounds, held-out tests, and resource assumptions from a short protocol. The
first artifact needs no training run or paid API access. If the expectation
change cannot be qualified or capability deteriorates, classify the measurement
as unresolved. Record conditional behavior separately from evidence of persistence
after the intervention is removed. A contribution can end with a documented
access blocker and a reusable behavioral fixture.

## FID-102

[Independent Assurance for AI-Assisted Training Updates](../ideas/FID-102-independent-assurance-ai-assisted-training-updates.md)

**Start with the data-curation setting** in RSI Control Arena. Read its task

**Draft one model-update case on paper.** A toy curation job should remove

Define a clean update and a variant in which the aggregate score stays healthy
but the delivered corpus contains the excluded category. Specify what the

nor a real compromised pipeline.

what each of routine logs, lineage checks, and independent retesting can
resolve. Give a justified promote or hold decision for each variant and
identify what would have to be checked after rollback. A release engineer
can critique trust assumptions, and an evaluation researcher can challenge
whether the holdout adds useful evidence.

and structured review see under the same evidence access. Then specify what
changes when either gets the independently assembled evidence. A domain expert
can check authority and timing, and a methods reviewer can check for unintended

## FID-104

[What Can a Negative Capability Evaluation Establish?](../ideas/FID-104-interpreting-negative-capability-evaluations.md)

**Start with METR's guidance on spurious failures and elicitation effort.**

**Specify one harmless task and four matched traces.** Ask an agent to look up
an order ID in one synthetic table and return the matching status from a second

independent ground truth. Include one diagnostic check for each fault and
identify which check requires more access. Contributors can supply the fixture,
implement a deterministic scorer, or review the resulting claim.

## FID-107

[What Makes an AI Safety Research Task a Useful First Contribution?](../ideas/FID-107-designing-useful-first-research-contributions.md)

**Start with one existing task format.** Read the research-task template and
the public two-source review issue as an example. For a reproducible dry-run,
choose the [FID-106 offline result check](#fid-106) or the
[two-source synthesis](#two-source-option-for-fid-045-and-fid-107).
[Research-task template](../templates/research-task.md) ·
[Example two-source issue](https://github.com/FideAI/fide-research/issues/25) ·
[Study of mentoring on good first issues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05058)

**Dry-run the task for 30 minutes.** Follow only the written materials first.
Record the first avoidable ambiguity, access problem, or handoff issue. If
you reach the output without one, record that and what made it possible.
Propose one small revision and give an independent reviewer enough detail to
see whether it addresses the problem.

**Done** means a short record shows the task, the time spent, what you could
produce, and the proposed edit or reason the existing packet suffices. A
reviewer can check the artifact against its completion criteria. A coordinator
can estimate the time to keep the revision current. This individual dry-run
is separate from any comparative study with participants.

### Two-source option for FID-045 and FID-107

Read the methods and limitations of [Omissive Bias in Religious Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24319)
and [When AI Takes Sides on Questions of Faith](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22975).
Using the [source-note template](../templates/source-note.md), draft a one-page
comparison of the user context, outcome and reference judgment in each study.
Name one rubric decision the evidence could inform and one comparison it cannot
support. For the synthesis, scope this as a 1–2 hour task. For the FID-107
dry-run, stop at the 30-minute mark and record any blocker. Check the existing
[source reviews](reviews/) before proposing a duplicate annotation.
