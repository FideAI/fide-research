# Claims Policy

This repository contains research calls and a catalog of released research.
Calls hold hypotheses, project briefs and collaboration requests. Publication
entries point to specific reports, papers and artifacts with explicit limitations.

Do not cite an idea brief as a Fide AI finding.

Fide AI findings require a separate public artifact such as:

- a technical report;
- a benchmark release;
- a paper;
- a public field brief;
- a leaderboard snapshot with methodology and limitations.

## Allowed Claims

It is acceptable to say:

- "Fide AI is scoping this research idea."
- "This idea is open for collaboration."
- "This brief proposes a possible evaluation method."

## Disallowed Claims

Do not say:

- "Fide AI has validated this result."
- "This model is safe for faith-facing use."
- "This benchmark proves pastoral competence."
- "This idea represents official doctrine or institutional endorsement."

unless a separate reviewed Fide AI publication explicitly supports that claim.

## Publication records

Only register publicly released work. A preprint is not peer-reviewed merely
because it appears in this catalog. A report or brief can contribute original
analysis without being an experiment; describe its method accurately.

Catalog claims must be supported by the linked work. State what evidence was
released and what can be reproduced. Keep corrections and withdrawals visible,
with dated explanations and links. Listing a completed publication does not
imply that the broader research question has been resolved.
